00 / Why this site exists

Three reasons this exists now

One book, one policy shift, and one thing neuroscience keeps confirming. All three point the same direction. You are old enough to use AI for schoolwork, but nobody has taught you how to do it without handing over the wheel, or your own brain.

The spark

"How to Talk to AI (And How Not To)"

Jamie Bartlett, the author who inspired this site

Bartlett's argument is simple: hundreds of millions of people now talk to AI every day, but almost nobody was taught how. Some people use it to think better. Others quietly hand over their judgement to it. What matters is whether you stay in control of the conversation, not which tool you open. This whole site rebuilds that idea for a classroom.

The science

Copy paste builds nothing

Learning happens when your brain does the work: struggling with an idea, drafting your own attempt, comparing it against something else, arguing back. That struggle is what forms new neural connections. Reading an AI answer and pasting it straight into your work skips that step entirely, so your brain never builds the pathway it needed to build. Used this way, AI quietly trains you to stop thinking rather than to think better. Question every answer, and you keep the learning. Accept it silently, and you lose it.

Could I answer this myself in under 10 seconds?

01 / The rule under all 13 rules

You hold the wheel. Always.

A

Treat every answer as a draft.

You still have to judge it, edit it, and decide what actually belongs in your essay, your code, or your argument.

B

Your reasoning comes first.

Try the hard part yourself before you ask. If AI does your thinking, you don't get better at thinking.

C

Keep some distance on purpose.

Staying formal and direct with it keeps you thinking for yourself, instead of trusting it the way you would trust a friend.

04 / Where you stand

Why VGS is different

The new Udir guidance treats AI access as something that grows with you, not a switch that is either fully on or fully off.

TRINN 1 TO 7

Not yet

Generative AI is mostly kept out of the classroom while foundational skills, like reading, writing and arithmetic, are still forming.

TRINN 8 TO 10

Careful and gradual

AI is introduced step by step, only after teachers themselves are trained enough to guide its use well.

You are here
VIDEREGÅENDE

Purposeful use, your responsibility

You are expected to use AI sensibly for schoolwork and to prepare for further study and work, which is exactly why this site exists.
